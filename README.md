# 🌾 फ़asal Sarthi – Crop Disease Detection & Farmer Support Website

**फ़asal Sarthi** is a companion web platform for the **फ़asal Sarthi Mobile App**, designed to empower farmers with technology-driven crop health insights and agricultural support.  
The website serves as an informative and outreach platform — showcasing what it is, how it works, and how it benefits the farming community.

---

## 🧭 Project Overview

The **फ़asal Sarthi Website** acts as an official landing page for the **Crop Disease Detection and Farmer Support Application**.  
Its main goal is to **educate**, **engage**, and **build trust** among users (especially farmers and stakeholders) by providing clear information about the app, its mission, and success stories.

---

## 🌐 Key Features

### 🏠 Home Page
- Introduces **फ़asal Sarthi** with a clean and modern layout.
- Explains the **purpose** and **core technology** behind the crop disease detection system.
- Contains clear **Call-to-Action (CTA)** buttons for app download or contact.

### 🧑‍🌾 About Us
- Provides background information about the **project, developers, and mission**.
- Explains how **फ़asal Sarthi** contributes to **sustainable agriculture and rural empowerment**.

### 💡 How It Works
- Step-by-step overview of how the app helps farmers:
    1. Upload crop images.
    2. ML model detects diseases.
    3. Farmers get instant insights and recommendations.

### 💬 Testimonials
- Displays real **reviews and feedback** from farmers who have benefited from the app.
- Enhances credibility and community trust.

### 📞 Contact / Support
- Simple form or details for users to reach out for **technical or informational support**.
